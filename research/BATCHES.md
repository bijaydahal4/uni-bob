# Research batches

Uni list = Russell Group minus Oxford & Cambridge (russellgroup.ac.uk, checked 2026-10-17) + Bath + Loughborough
+ St Andrews + every England/Wales uni ranked roughly top 75 (<= 77) in the Complete University Guide 2027
overall table (published 2 June 2026) that offers a relevant course, + SOAS (named in the brief, CUG 94th).
Left out: Oxford, Cambridge; Scottish/NI unis other than Edinburgh, Glasgow, QUB, St Andrews; specialist arts
and agricultural institutions without general Economics/Business courses (University of the Arts London 27,
Harper Adams 38, Norwich University of the Arts 60, Falmouth 65, Arts University Bournemouth 72).
